# xay loves or

## 题目描述

xay loves or. He gives you x and s, you need to calculate how many positive integer y satisfy $x \operatorname{or} y=s$ .

## 输入描述:

The first line contains two positive integers x and s .

It's guaranteed that $1\le x,s<2^{31}$ .

## 输出描述:

Print the number of y satisfy $x \operatorname{or} y=s$ .

## 示例1

输入

```
2 5
```

输出

```
0
```
